    How it works

    Three seconds from thought to done.

    No new app to learn. It sits in your menu bar (Mac) or system tray (Windows) and waits for you.

    1

    Wake it, two ways

    Hold a key on your keyboard while you talk (pick any key you like), or say the wake phrase hands-free.

    2

    Talk normally, several steps in one go if you like. A small panel shows what it heard.

    “Open Chrome, go to my CRM, click Contacts”
    3

    Every step is shown live. Press the same key, hit Esc or say “stop” and it stops instantly.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Does it work on Mac and Windows?

    Yes, both. On Mac it needs Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later. On Windows it needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Your plan covers both, so you can use it on your Mac and your Windows PC.

    Do I have to say “Flow Command, activate” every time?

    No. You can simply hold a key on your keyboard, speak, and let go. You choose the key in settings: right ⌘, Ctrl, Fn, F5 or any combination. Use the wake phrase when your hands are busy, the key when you'd rather stay quiet, or both.

    Will it understand my accent?

    Yes. It uses Whisper, a speech model trained on hundreds of thousands of hours of speech in many accents, and it runs right on your computer. It's especially good with Indian English, where most voice tools struggle.

    Is it safe to let it control my computer?

    You approve each permission yourself during setup. It only acts after you wake it, shows every step live, asks before it sends, pays, deletes or posts anything, and never types passwords or codes. The same key that wakes it stops it instantly.

    What gets sent to the internet?

    Your voice is turned into text on your computer. Simple commands never leave it. Bigger tasks send the task and the text of the page you're on (occasionally a screenshot, when a page can't be read as text) to the AI brain to work out the next step. Turn on Private mode and nothing leaves your computer at all.

    Do I need my own AI subscription?

    No. Simple commands need no AI at all, and Pro includes the AI brain. Power users can plug in their own Claude key if they prefer.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes, monthly and yearly plans cancel in one click and you keep Pro until the end of what you've paid for.
